# WPlayer Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 25 October 2026

WPlayer is a file manager, media player and image viewer for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

WPlayer does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything it does happens on your device.

## What the app accesses on your device

To work, WPlayer reads files that are already on your device:

- **Photos, videos and audio** (Android "Photos and videos" / "Music and audio" permissions): to show your media library, play videos and music, and show images.
- **All files access** (optional, Android "All files access"): only if you allow it from the Files tab, to browse folders, open documents, clean up storage (large, old and duplicate files, APKs and empty folders) and recover deleted files. The rest of the app works without it.

These files are read on your device only. They are never uploaded or sent anywhere.

## What the app stores on your device

- **Settings and playback history:** your preferences, the position you stopped at in each video, and recently opened files. These are saved in the app's private storage on your device.
- **Private Vault:** files you move into the vault are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key held in your device's Android Keystore, and protected by a PIN you choose. Vault files and keys are excluded from Android backup and device transfer, so they never leave your device. If you uninstall the app or forget your PIN, vault files cannot be recovered.

Android may include the app's other settings in your own Google device backup if you have backup turned on. That backup is controlled by Android and your Google account, not by WPlayer.

## Changes to files

WPlayer only deletes, renames, moves or edits files when you ask it to. Android shows its own confirmation for these actions where it requires one.

## Children

WPlayer is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
