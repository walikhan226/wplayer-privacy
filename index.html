Official Policy

WPlayer Privacy Policy

📅 Effective date: 25 October 2026 • Package: com.wplayer.app • Target: Android 8.0 - 15

Summary

WPlayer is a file manager, media player, and image viewer for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Key Guarantee: WPlayer does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal data. The app has no internet permission, no user accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything it does happens strictly on your local device.

What the App Accesses on Your Device

To function, WPlayer reads files stored on your local device:

  • Photos, videos and audio: (Android READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): Used to display your media folders, stream video, play audio tracks, and display photos in the gallery.
  • All files access: (Optional Android MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Only requested when explicitly opened from the Files tab after an in-app explanation. Used to browse arbitrary non-media directories, open office documents, perform storage cleanup (large, old, duplicate files, APKs, empty folders), and recover deleted files. The media tabs remain fully functional without this permission.

These files are processed on your device only. They are never uploaded or transmitted over any network.

What the App Stores on Your Device

  • Settings and playback history: User preferences, playback resume positions (timestamps), watched indicators, and recently opened file references are stored exclusively in the app's private sandbox storage on your device.
  • Private Vault: Files and folders moved into the vault are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using cryptographic keys generated and stored in your device's hardware Android Keystore, protected by your custom 4-digit PIN. Vault contents and cryptographic keys are explicitly excluded from Android auto-backup and device migration, ensuring they never leave your device.

Changes to Files

WPlayer only deletes, renames, moves, or edits files when explicitly requested by you. Android system confirmation prompts are triggered for all operations that require explicit user approval under Scoped Storage standards.

Children's Privacy

WPlayer is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not collect any personal information from any user.

Changes to this Policy

If this policy changes, updated revisions will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.
