Privacy Policy

Application: WPlayer - Media & Files (Package: com.wplayer.app)
Effective Date: October 24, 2026 • Version 1.0.0

Executive Summary

WPlayer is an entirely offline, privacy-first multimedia player and local storage management tool for Android. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information, media content, usage logs, device identifiers, or analytics whatsoever. All operations take place strictly on your device.

1. Zero Data Collection & Zero Telemetry

WPlayer operates with no backend servers, no analytics SDKs (no Firebase, no Crashlytics, no Google Analytics), and zero advertising frameworks. The application does not contain code to track user identity, device location, hardware IDs, browsing habits, or playback history.

2. Android Permissions & How They Are Used

WPlayer adheres strictly to Google Play developer policies and the principle of least privilege. Permissions are never requested until the user navigates to a feature that requires them.

Permission Purpose Required / Optional
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO Enables indexing and playback of local video files. Requested only when opening the Videos tab. Feature-gated
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Enables browsing and editing of local photos. Requested only when opening the Photos tab. Feature-gated
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO Enables scanning and playback of local audio files. Requested only when opening the Audio tab. Feature-gated
MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Enables directory traversal, APK installation, archive extraction, and deep junk cleaning in the dedicated Files and Clean up tabs. The core media tabs remain fully functional even if this permission is permanently declined. Optional (Files tab only)
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Enables media playback controls (play/pause/skip) in the Android notification shade and lock screen while playing in the background. Optional
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Ensures uninterrupted audio playback when the screen is turned off or when multitasking. System Core

3. Private Vault & Cryptographic Protection

Files imported into the Private Vault are encrypted locally using industry-standard AES-256 (GCM mode) with a user-supplied PIN. Encryption keys are derived using PBKDF2 with a unique salt generated on-device. Neither the developer nor any third party can recover your encrypted files if you forget your PIN, as keys are never transmitted off the device.

4. Storage Clean Up & Safety Guarantee

The Clean up feature analyzes local storage caches, empty directories, duplicated files, and obsolete APKs:

  • Zero automatic deletion: Scanning requires manual initiation via the "Scan" button.
  • User-verified action: Files are never deleted without explicit confirmation by the user.
  • Safe categories: Scans target temporary app caches and user-selected obsolete files only.

5. Third-Party Libraries & Dependencies

WPlayer is built using open-source, official Android Jetpack and Media3 libraries. No third-party network, advertising, tracking, or telemetry libraries are bundled into the release APK.

6. Children's Privacy

WPlayer is not directed at children under the age of 13 and collects zero personal data from any person of any age.

7. Updates to this Policy

Any revisions to this privacy policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
